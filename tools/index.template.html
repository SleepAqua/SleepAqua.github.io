nav-smoothing-forensics

Case study

A NAV-only forensic test on an anonymised fixed-income wealth product: 97 observations over 145 days, no holdings and no prospectus — one internal-consistency identity. If the NAV accrues honestly, the change over a multi-day gap equals the sum of the daily accruals. It does not, and the gap is concentrated.
