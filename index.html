Turns a file of trade prints into OHLCV bars one session at a time. The awkward parts — hard session boundaries and ticks that arrive late — are the whole point; everything else is small enough to read in an afternoon. Stdlib Python, ~260k ticks/s.

nav-smoothing-forensics

Case study

A NAV-only forensic test on an anonymised fixed-income wealth product: 97 observations over 145 days, no holdings and no prospectus — one internal-consistency identity. If the NAV accrues honestly, the change over a multi-day gap equals the sum of the daily accruals. It does not, and the gap is concentrated.

mahjong-mc

Simulation

Four-player Japanese Mahjong, played millions of times so strategies can be compared instead of argued about: win, self-draw, deal-in, riichi and average placement side by side, on a ruleset with furiten, chankan and han/fu scoring. Pure Python, no dependencies, every core.

GapFiller

Simulation

Monte Carlo Yahtzee under full standard rules, with the upper-section and Yahtzee bonuses: 20,000 games per strategy and a standard error on every mean, 138.19 ± 0.28 for the best against 111.60 ± 0.20 for random play. A solved optimal-play baseline is included for comparison.
